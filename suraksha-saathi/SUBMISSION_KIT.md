## Form fields (copy-paste)
**Title (93/100):**
Suraksha Saathi: Multilingual AI Scam Shield for Elders and First-Time Digital Users in India

**Theme:** Digital Safety & Cyber Fraud Awareness

**Theme alignment + SDGs (435/500):**
Digital fraud (digital-arrest, KYC, UPI-collect, lottery scams) hits elders and first-time digital users hardest and erodes trust in digital India. Suraksha Saathi gives them an AI safety net in their own language, directly serving Digital Safety & Cyber Fraud Awareness while advancing digital inclusion. SDGs: 16 (16.4, curbing illicit financial flows), 10 (protecting vulnerable groups), 9 (9.c, safe and affordable digital access).

**Problem (456/500):**
Scammers pose as police, banks or relatives and create panic so victims pay before thinking. Elders, rural users and first-time smartphone users are most affected: they read mostly regional languages, cannot spot fake links or UPI collect requests, and have no one to ask in the moment. Existing tools are English-first, need technical knowledge, or act only after the money is lost. The gap is instant, simple, multilingual help at the moment of the scam.

**Approach (488/500):**
Users forward a message, upload a screenshot or share a call recording. OCR and speech-to-text convert it to text; an Indic-language NLP classifier plus rules for links, UPI IDs and urgency phrases detect the scam type. The app replies with a risk score, a simple explanation by text and voice in Tamil, Hindi or English, and next steps (call 1930, report at cybercrime.gov.in). A family member can be alerted. Stack: Python, FastAPI, Tesseract, Whisper, React PWA. Impact: fewer victims.
